## [Conversation: Building Spaced Repetition into e-learning platforms](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7xdCHaJdA4U)

Trey Hunner, Sheena O'Connell, and Keith Murray discuss Spaced Repetition and interesting ways it can be applied in E-Learning platforms.

Building off of a recent project focused on developing a Learning Management System Sheena had made, 
Trey's recent implementation of Free Spaced Repetition,
and the [Reading Club's](../reading-club/index.md) recent review of "Retrieval Practice" in the [Big Book of Computing Pedagogy](../../resources/big-book-of-computing-pedagogy.md)
the discussion explores using Free Spaced Repetition as a way to identify lessons a Learning Management System could prioritize.

A reoccurring element was trying to teach someone who has some unknown amount of background knowledge, 
and the struggle around trying to make sure they know what is needed, while not loosing their engagement by spending too much time on topics they have a solid foundation in.
