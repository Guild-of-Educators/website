# The Big Book of Computing Pedagogy

**By:** Raspberry Pi Foundation (Hello World magazine)
**Link:** <https://helloworld.raspberrypi.org/books/big_book_of_pedagogy>

## What it is

The Big Book of Computing Pedagogy is the Raspberry Pi Foundations collection of short pieces which focus on computing in the classroom. 
Each section is largely independent of the rest, is short (often two pages), making it easy to read without needing large blocks of time or focus and is written to emphasize how to apply the concepts to the classroom.
The sections are sorted into the Raspberry Pi Foundation's "Twelve Principles of Computing Pedagogy", and have many outgoing links for deeper dives into the theory behind the presented concept.

## Why we recommend it

The book is built as bite-sized concepts with an emphasis on classroom application, rather than deep dives into theory. 
This focus helps to quickly explore a concept and apply it in the classroom, while having what's needed to go deeper linked as cited material.

## Good for

This is great for anyone interested in "How" to teach computing. 
It has many examples which are quickly adaptable to a variety of classroom types, learner levels, and demographics.
